# Ethical Use and Responsible Communication

This project analyzes aggregate counts related to unaccompanied children, a vulnerable population. Technical quality does not remove the need for child-centered safeguards and accountable human judgment.

## Acceptable uses

- Learning and demonstrating reproducible analytics engineering.
- Reviewing aggregate historical patterns with explicit provenance warnings.
- Testing data-quality, forecasting, monitoring, and dashboard code.
- Comparing research methods under a documented evaluation protocol.

## Prohibited or unsupported uses

- Decisions about an individual child, sponsor, placement, custody, release, or service.
- Automated staffing, contracting, bed-allocation, enforcement, or funding directives.
- Claims that the included file or forecast is official HHS/CBP information.
- Causal conclusions from correlations or feature importance.
- Public reporting without verifying source rights, definitions, revisions, and provenance.

## Review checklist for any applied extension

1. Replace the source with documented, authorized, authoritative data.
2. Conduct privacy, civil-rights, welfare, legal, and information-security review.
3. Validate definitions and missingness with program experts.
4. Evaluate error and uncertainty across relevant facilities, geographies, and time regimes.
5. Define who can act on an output, what human review is required, and how decisions are appealed.
6. Monitor freshness, drift, failures, and disparate impact; retain an interpretable fallback.
7. Publish limitations as prominently as results.

The software is an independent research prototype and is not affiliated with or endorsed by HHS, CBP, or another government agency.
